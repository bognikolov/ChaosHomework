HW13 - Optimizations 01
Scene: scene0.crtscene, 4014 triangles, 1920x1080, bucket_size=24 (from scene file)
Test machine: 1 CPU core

baseline (single thread, no AABB, whole image as one bucket): 338.27s
+ scene AABB:                                                  184.30s   (45.5% faster than baseline)
+ multithreaded buckets (bucket_size from scene, all cores):   183.80s   (no further gain: test machine has only 1 core)

All three renders are byte-identical (verified via MD5) - the optimizations
only change render time, not the output image.

On a multi-core machine, the multithreaded-buckets step is expected to give
a further ~Nx speedup proportional to core count, since bucket work is
independent and evenly distributed across threads via a shared atomic
work-queue index.
