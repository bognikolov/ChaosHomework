HW14 - Optimizations 02: acceleration tree (BVH)
Test machine: 1 CPU core (same as HW13 measurements)

BVH: median-split binary tree, split axis = longest axis of the node's bounding
box, leaf threshold = 4 triangles, built once per render before tracing starts.
"Before" numbers below already include the AABB + multithreading optimizations
from HW13 (i.e. this measures the BVH's improvement on top of the previous
best setup, not from scratch).

scene0.crtscene - 8 triangles, 1920x1080
  before (flat AABB scan): 0.653s
  after  (BVH):            0.571s
  (negligible geometry count, BVH build overhead roughly cancels the gain)

scene1.crtscene - 4014 triangles, 1920x1080
  before (flat AABB scan): 185.13s
  after  (BVH):            2.92s
  speedup: ~63x

Both renders (before/after) are pixel-identical except for ~200 pixels out of
2+ million along a single edge where two coplanar triangles from adjacent
Cornell-box walls tie at the same intersection distance - which of the two
wins is an ordering artifact independent of BVH vs. linear scan, not a
correctness bug (verified by direct A/B render comparison with the same code).
